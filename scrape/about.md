## Our Story
We started Rawasi Adventures with one purpose: to bring Muslim men into the wilderness. Not just for the adventure, but for what happens when you disconnect from everything and stand in front of Allah's creation with nothing between you and it.
In July 2026, we took our first group of brothers deep into the Sierra Nevada. We hiked through mountain terrain, swam in alpine lakes, camped in a stunning meadow, bonded over the campfire and prayed together. Brothers who started as strangers left as friends.
Today we run guided backpacking retreats built around that experience: professional guides, everything taken care of, and a personal call with every brother before the trip to get him ready. No experience needed. Just the intention to show up.
[BUTTON: Read our story]
Our mission is for every brother to come home with new brothers, new skills and a deeper connection to Allah's creation.

### Our Founders

#### Ibrahim Abdul-haqq Twist
Ibrahim was born into a family rooted in the outdoors, working in conservation in Ecuador's Amazon rainforest since 1995 and organizing nature retreats for the Bay Area Muslim community since 2008. His love for backpacking took hold on his first trip as a kid, on South Sister in Oregon. A California native, he backpacks the Sierra Nevada, chases trout and salmon as a backcountry fisherman, and loves studying maps to plan every route. He has also explored the Amazon jungle, volcanoes in Indonesia, and trails in New Zealand, Australia, Italy, Utah and Oregon.
[BUTTON: Read more]

#### Imam Yaseen Mahmoud
A Bay Area native and Eagle Scout, Yaseen brings a lifelong connection to the outdoors and a deep commitment to community. He holds a degree in Islamic Law and Theology from Zaytuna College, where his studies focused on Arabic Literature, Islamic Jurisprudential Law and Ethics.

Rawasi (رَوَاسِي) means the mountains Allah set firmly into the earth. We take brothers into them, along the rivers and paths, and they come home standing firmer.

### Designed around you
Every retreat is designed to push you somewhere new and unlock something you didn't know you had in you. You'll find out you're capable of doing something hard.

#### Ready, whatever your background
No experience needed. Every brother who books schedules a personal call with our team, and we help you prepare and get trail-ready before the trip.

#### The most beautiful places
We take you deep into the wilderness to see Allah's creation up close, places most people only see in photos.

#### Professional from start to finish
A carefully chosen team of guides, a safety and emergency plan, and every detail prepared, so you can be fully present.

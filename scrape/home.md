### The backcountry is serious. We plan for it.
Going with experienced guides is what makes the wilderness accessible. Every Rawasi trip is planned around keeping brothers safe, so you can focus on the experience.
- Wilderness First Aid certified guides Trained to respond to injuries and illness far from help.
- Emergency plans and satellite connection A satellite device keeps us in contact with emergency services, even with no cell service.
- Route and weather planning We study every route and the weather history before the trip, and monitor conditions throughout.
- Close attention in a small group 3 guides for 12 brothers, keeping an eye on everyone's health and pace.
- Wildlife protocols Food stays in bear canisters, and guides are trained for bear encounters.

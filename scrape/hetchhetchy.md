### Itinerary: 4 Days, 3 Nights

#### Shuttle Out, Gear Check and Campfire Feast
Meet at MCC East Bay for a 10:30 AM departure, with pickups available along the route, and ride to Yosemite together in our AWD Mercedes Sprinter. At camp, your guide fits your pack and gear to you, then we sit down to a primal feast of halal steak and potatoes by the fire.

#### Into the Wilderness: Waterfalls and Granite Walls
Early start, packs on. We backpack about 6.7 miles along the Hetch Hetchy Reservoir, past Kolana Rock, towering granite walls and the cascades of Wapama Falls, to base camp near Rancheria Falls. We set up camp and enjoy a roaring campfire under the winter stars.

#### Explore from Base Camp
A lighter day, with camp staying put. Learn how to catch and cook food in the wild in a group activity led by your lead wilderness guide, Ibrahim Twist. Catch the sunset over the valley, then close the night stargazing under Yosemite's dark sky.

#### Hike Out and Celebrate
After Fajr, we pack up camp and backpack the same trail out, about 6.7 miles. Every brother who finishes receives his Winter Trekker completion patch, then we ride home together in the Sprinter, back at MCC East Bay Monday evening.

### Is This Trip Right for Me?
No backpacking experience needed. There are two backpacking days, with a day at base camp in between and an optional day hike. If you're in basic shape, you can do this. For the most enjoyable trip, we recommend a little training beforehand to build your pace.

#### We'll Get You Ready
Every brother who books schedules a personal call with our team. We'll learn where you're at, answer your questions, and help you get trail-ready before January. On the trail, 3 guides look after 12 participants, so you're supported every step of the way.

### Your Guides

#### Ibrahim Abdul-haqq Twist
Ibrahim was born into a family rooted in the outdoors, working in conservation in Ecuador's Amazon rainforest since 1995 and organizing nature retreats for the Bay Area Muslim community since 2008, a life that instilled in him a love and respect for the wild. His love for backpacking took hold on his first trip as a kid, on South Sister in Oregon. A California native, he backpacks the Sierra Nevada, chases trout and salmon as a backcountry fisherman, hunts, and loves studying maps to plan every route. He has also explored the Amazon jungle, volcanoes in Indonesia, and trails in New Zealand, Australia, Italy, Utah and Oregon. As co-founder of Rawasi Adventures, his passion is bringing Muslim brothers into the wilderness for life-changing experiences, far from the noise of everyday life.

#### Yasir Malang
Yasir has spent years leading mountain bike rides, day hikes and group backpacking trips to build community and help brothers connect with Allah's creation. Whether it's your first night under the stars or you're a seasoned hiker, he's there to make sure you feel confident and comfortable in the wild.

#### Bushran Mohammed
Bushran found the outdoors in high school and made it a way of life in Colorado, backpacking to alpine lakes, mountain biking and snowboarding. Now in California, he says yes to almost any adventure, from cliff jumping to midnight kayaking in the Everglades, and he loves bringing people into the wilderness for the first time. Out there, he prays in the open air and takes in what Allah has created.

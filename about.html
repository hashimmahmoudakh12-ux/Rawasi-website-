About

Connect to Allah by connecting to His creation.

Rawasi Adventures is a California-based community leading guided outdoor adventures for Muslim men.

Our Story

Bring Muslim men into the wilderness.

We started Rawasi Adventures with one purpose: to bring Muslim men into the wilderness. Not just for the adventure, but for what happens when you disconnect from everything and stand in front of Allah's creation with nothing between you and it.

In July 2026, we took our first group of brothers deep into the Sierra Nevada. We hiked through mountain terrain, swam in alpine lakes, camped in a stunning meadow, bonded over the campfire and prayed together. Brothers who started as strangers left as friends.

Today we run guided backpacking retreats built around that experience: professional guides, everything taken care of, and a personal call with every brother before the trip to get him ready. No experience needed. Just the intention to show up.

Our mission is for every brother to come home with new brothers, new skills and a deeper connection to Allah's creation.

رواسي

Why we’re called Rawasi.

As explained by Imam Yaseen Mahmoud.

Where our name comes from

وَأَلْقَىٰ فِي الْأَرْضِ رَوَاسِيَ أَن تَمِيدَ بِكُمْ وَأَنْهَارًا وَسُبُلًا لَّعَلَّكُمْ تَهْتَدُونَ

“And He has cast into the earth firmly set mountains, lest it shift with you, and rivers and paths, so that you may be guided.”Surah An-Nahl, 16:15

Rawasi (رَوَاسِي) means the mountains Allah set firmly into the earth. We take brothers into them, along the rivers and paths, and they come home standing firmer.

01

The meaning

Rawasi translates to lofty, stabilizing mountains.

02

The sign

The Qur'an describes mountains as rawasi — firmly set anchors stabilizing the earth. In 1855, geologist George Airy confirmed mountains have deep roots extending into the earth — something impossible to observe in the time of the Prophet ﷺ.

03

What it represents

Just as mountains stabilize the earth, we want to be a firmly fixed community: Muslims stabilizing each other through companionship and connecting with Allah.

Behind the scenes

Our guides train hard so you don’t have to.

On our advanced guide-training trip in a remote section of the Sierra Nevada, the team practiced off-trail navigation, class 2–3 scrambles and high-elevation summits.

“When we reach the summit we thank Allah for safe access up the mountain.”
